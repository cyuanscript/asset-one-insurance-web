V0 · Goal filter Interactive

What should the policy do?

Term life

Coverage for a set number of years, often 10, 20 or 30.

Whole life

Lifelong coverage with a level premium and guaranteed cash value.

Indexed universal life

Lifelong coverage with flexible premiums and index-linked growth.

Survivorship life

One policy on two people that pays after the second death.

V4 · IUL tab

Where each premium goes

Charges come out every month, even in years when no interest is credited.

1You pay a premium
2Monthly charges come out: the cost of insurance and policy fees
3The rest goes to your cash value, split between index options and a fixed account
4Each year, interest is credited based on the index, between the floor and the cap

V7 · Where it fits

Life insurance or an annuity?

They protect against opposite risks, and many plans use both.

If life is shorter than planned

Life insurance pays the people you leave behind.

If life is longer than planned

An annuity can turn savings into income for a set period or for life.
